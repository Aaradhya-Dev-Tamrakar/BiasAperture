📖 How to Read These Metrics (In Plain English)

Disparate Impact Ratio (DIR)

Selection Rate Ratio / 80% Rule

Compares the positive prediction rate of a demographic group against the highest-selected group. Under the US EEOC 4/5ths (80%) rule, if the highest group is selected 80% of the time, all other groups should be selected at least 64% of the time (80% of 80%). A ratio below 0.80 indicates adverse impact.

Ideal = 1.00; Safe threshold ≥ 0.80 • EEOC 4/5ths Rule / EU AI Act Annex IV §2(g)

Demographic Parity Difference (DPD)

Positive Rate Percentage Gap

Measures whether the model favors one group over another in raw volume of positive predictions. A difference of 0.15 means individuals from one group are 15 percentage points more likely to receive a positive prediction than individuals from another group.

Ideal = 0.00; Safe threshold ≤ 0.10 • EU AI Act Art. 10(2)(f) / NIST AI RMF Measure 2.11

Equalized Odds Difference (EOD)

Error Rate Equality Gap

Ensures the model is equally accurate for all demographics. It verifies that qualified individuals have an equal chance of being accepted, and unqualified individuals have an equal chance of being rejected, regardless of identity.

Ideal = 0.00; Safe threshold ≤ 0.10 • EU AI Act Art. 10(2)(g) / NIST AI RMF Measure 2.11

Equal Opportunity Difference (EOP)

True Positive Opportunity Gap

Focuses strictly on true positive outcomes (e.g. qualified applicants or correct identity matches). A difference of 0.08 means qualified individuals in one group are 8 percentage points less likely to be correctly recognized.
