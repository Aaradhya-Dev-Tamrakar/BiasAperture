## 3. Detailed Accomplishments & Technical Highlights

### 3.1 20-Track Parallel Research Sprint & 3-Level Syntheses

- Executed 20 targeted research investigations covering demographic taxonomies, intersectional binning, metric trade-offs, statistical power, bootstrap validity, surrogate modeling, and regulatory compliance.
- Synthesized findings into three authoritative specification documents:
  - [`HIGH_LEVEL_SYNTHESIS.md`](../../docs/research/HIGH_LEVEL_SYNTHESIS.md): Executive summary and statutory mapping.
  - [`MID_LEVEL_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../../docs/research/MID_LEVEL_ARCHITECTURE.md): Component interactions, data flow, and interface contracts.
  - [`LOW_LEVEL_SPECIFICATION.md`](../../docs/research/LOW_LEVEL_SPECIFICATION.md): Mathematical equations, algorithmic bounds, and error handling.
- Formalized the auditable [`CLAIM_LEDGER.md`](../../docs/research/CLAIM_LEDGER.md) tracking 60+ empirical assertions through `ASSERTED` $\to$ `VERIFIED` $\to$ `REPRODUCIBLE` states.

### 3.2 Dataset Profiling & Empirical Discoveries

- Authored exploration scripts [`scripts/explore_fairface.py`](../../scripts/explore_fairface.py) and [`scripts/explore_utkface.py`](../../scripts/explore_utkface.py).
- Uncovered that FairFace contains **97,698 released images** on disk across train/validation splits, correcting paper citations of 108.5k.
- Discovered systematic DEX age label noise and label boundary artifacts in UTKFace, providing concrete empirical justification for designating UTKFace as a secondary validation testbed rather than primary benchmark.

### 3.3 Native AIF360 Backend Harmonization (`src/bias_aperture/fairness/backends.py`)

- Replaced adapter pass-through with native `aif360.datasets.BinaryLabelDataset` and `aif360.metrics.ClassificationMetric`.
- Successfully harmonized metric definitions between Fairlearn and AIF360 to ensure consistent cross-backend validation.

### 3.4 Exact Additive Shapley Surrogate Explainability (`src/bias_aperture/explainability.py`)

- Implemented linear surrogate feature attribution computing exact Shapley values:
  $$\phi_i = w_i (x_i - \mathbb{E}[x_i])$$
- Built defensive import and execution wrappers safeguarding against Windows C-extension DLL and MAX_PATH loading failures.
- Added automatic attribution triggers on statistically significant disparities ($p < 0.05, n \ge 30$).
