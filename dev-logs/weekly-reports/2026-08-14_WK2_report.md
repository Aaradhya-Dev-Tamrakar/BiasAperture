## 3. Detailed Accomplishments & Technical Highlights

### 3.1 Milestone M1 Schema Contract Formulation (`docs/schema-lock-m1.md`)
- Formalized the locked demographic schema taxonomy:
  - **Gender**: `Male`, `Female` (binary baseline matching benchmark annotations).
  - **Race / Ethnicity (7 categories)**: `White`, `Black`, `Latino_Hispanic`, `East Asian`, `Southeast Asian`, `Indian`, `Middle Eastern`.
  - **Age (9 categories)**: `0-2`, `3-9`, `10-19`, `20-29`, `30-39`, `40-49`, `50-59`, `60-69`, `70+`.
- Established the `SubjectRecord` schema:
  $$\text{SubjectRecord}(\text{subject\_id}, \text{image\_path}, \text{ground\_truth}, \text{prediction}, \text{probabilities}, \text{demographics}, \text{metadata})$$
- Formulated the statistical adequacy constraint in `MetricResult`:
  $$\text{If } n < 30 \implies \text{insufficient\_sample} = \text{True}, \quad \text{metric\_value} = \text{None}$$

### 3.2 Literature Review Matrix Scaffolding
- Extracted and cataloged claims across 9 foundational papers:
  1. *Gender Shades* (Buolamwini & Gebru, 2018) — Intersectional error disparities in commercial APIs.
  2. *FairFace* (Kärkkäinen & Joo, 2019/2021) — 7-race balanced benchmark and ResNet-34 baseline.
  3. *Model Cards for Model Reporting* (Mitchell et al., 2019) — Standardized reporting schema.
  4. *Datasheets for Datasets* (Gebru et al., 2021) — Dataset provenance and ethical documentation.
  5. *Equality of Opportunity in Supervised Learning* (Hardt et al., 2016) — Equalized odds & opportunity definitions.
  6. *Fairness and Machine Learning* (Barocas, Hardt, Narayanan, 2023) — Disparate impact & parity trade-offs.
  7. *A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions* (Lundberg & Lee, 2017) — SHAP attribution theory.
  8. *EU AI Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689)* — Article 10 (Data governance), Article 13 (Transparency), Article 15 (Accuracy/Robustness).
  9. *NIST AI Risk Management Framework (NIST AI 100-1)* — Map, Measure, Manage functions.
