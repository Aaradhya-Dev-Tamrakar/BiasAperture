# BiasAperture — Literature Review Matrix

Source: `report/references.bib` + `report/src/chapters/literatureReview.tex`. Excludes `euaiact2024` and `nist2023aiframework` (regulatory instruments, not research papers).

| Title / Author / Date | Conceptual Framework | Research Question(s) / Hypotheses | Datasets | Methodology | Analysis & Results | Conclusions | Implications for Future Research |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gender Shades: Intersectional Accuracy Disparities in Commercial Gender Classification**<br>Buolamwini & Gebru, 2018 | Intersectional auditing of commercial ML systems for demographic subgroup accuracy disparity | Do commercial gender-classification services perform equally across skin-tone × gender intersections? | Commercial gender-classification APIs (not a released dataset) | Empirical audit across intersectional subgroups (skin tone × gender) | Error rates as high as 34.7% for worst-performing intersectional subgroup vs. as low as 0.8% for best-performing | Commercial gender classifiers exhibit large, statistically significant intersectional disparities | Catalysed the fairness-auditing research agenda for computer vision; foundational citation for BiasAperture's motivation |
| **Fairness and Bias Mitigation in Computer Vision: A Survey**<br>Dehdashtian, Wang & Boddeti, 2024 | Taxonomic survey of bias sources and fairness/mitigation methods across the CV pipeline | Where does bias enter the CV pipeline, and what metric/mitigation families address each stage? | N/A (survey) | Literature taxonomy: data collection → annotation → training → deployment stages | Rich taxonomy of *what* to measure exists; comparatively little standardises *how* to measure it repeatably | Metrics/mitigation literature is mature; auditing-workflow literature is not | Identifies the exact engineering gap BiasAperture is built to close (reusable auditing pipeline vs. ad hoc audits) |
| **Equality of Opportunity in Supervised Learning**<br>Hardt, Price & Srebro, 2016 (NeurIPS) | Formal fairness definitions via error-rate parity | How should equalized odds and equal opportunity be formally defined and operationalised? | N/A (theoretical/formal) | Formal definition + derivation of post-processing correction | Equalized Odds = TPR + FPR parity across groups; Equal Opportunity = TPR parity only (positive class) | Neither definition subsumes the other; they answer different fairness questions | Directly operationalised as BiasAperture's EOD/EOP metrics (Core Four) |
| **The Four-Fifths Rule is Not Disparate Impact: A Woeful Tale of Epistemic Trespassing in Algorithmic Fairness**<br>Watkins, McKenna & Chen, 2022 | Critique of threshold-based fairness compliance ("epistemic trespassing") | Is the 80% four-fifths threshold, imported from employment law, a valid fairness bar for algorithmic systems? | N/A (legal/critical analysis) | Doctrinal + statistical critique of four-fifths rule origin and application | Treating DIR as pass/fail against an implicit threshold conflates legal compliance with actual fairness | Bare-ratio thresholds without statistical context are epistemically unjustified | Direct justification for FR-004/NFR-001: BiasAperture always pairs DIR with chi-squared test + bootstrap CI, never a bare ratio |
| **Where, Why, and How Is Bias Learned in Medical Image Analysis Models?**<br>Kurian et al., 2024 (eBioMedicine) | Proxy-variable / demographic encoding in CNNs via synthetic data | Can a CNN encode demographic information as a proxy even with no demographic label in training data? | Synthetic medical imaging data | Controlled synthetic-data study isolating proxy feature encoding (texture, lighting) | CNNs learn to exploit demographic info via unrelated, seemingly neutral visual features | Detected disparities cannot be assumed to arise only from the stratifying label — may be a proxy artefact | Direct justification for FR-005 (SHAP-based attribution on every flagged disparity) |
| **Model Cards for Model Reporting**<br>Mitchell et al., 2019 (FAT*) | Standardised model-documentation convention | How should a model's intended use, performance, and evaluation results be communicated to mixed technical/non-technical audiences? | N/A (documentation framework) | Proposed structured reporting template + case studies | Structured summary format improves legibility of model characteristics across stakeholder types | Documentation should be a standard artefact, not ad hoc | Structurally informs FR-006 (report generation) |
| **Datasheets for Datasets**<br>Gebru et al., 2018 (rev. 2021) | Standardised dataset-documentation convention | How should a dataset's motivation, composition, collection process, and limitations be documented? | N/A (documentation framework) | Proposed structured datasheet template | Structured documentation lets downstream users reason about dataset limitations | Datasets need the same documentation discipline as models | Structurally informs FR-006 alongside Model Cards; shapes dataset-provenance section of BiasAperture's report |
| **FairFace: Face Attribute Dataset for Balanced Race, Gender, and Age for Bias Measurement and Mitigation**<br>Karkkainen & Joo, 2021 (WACV) | Balanced benchmark dataset construction for bias measurement | Can a face dataset with balanced representation and a revised race taxonomy reduce classification skew seen in prior datasets? | FairFace (108,501 images; 7-category race taxonomy) | Dataset construction + balanced sampling methodology | Balanced representation across race/gender/age; 7-category taxonomy reduces skew vs. earlier datasets | A purpose-built balanced dataset materially improves subgroup-fairness measurement validity | BiasAperture's principal validation benchmark (FR-001, NFR-004 sizing target) |
| **Assessing High-Risk AI Systems under the EU AI Act: From Legal Requirements to Technical Verification**<br>Buscemi et al., 2025 | Legal-to-technical requirement decomposition for AI Act compliance | How can AI Act high-risk-system obligations be decomposed into concrete, technology-agnostic technical verification activities? | N/A (regulatory-technical mapping) | Taxonomy construction mapping legal articles → requirements → sub-requirements → verification activities | Produces a stable taxonomy with each obligation tied to a concrete technical check | Legal articles can be systematically operationalised into testable technical activities | Direct methodological template for BiasAperture's Article 10 mapping (FR-007, `sec:regmapping`) |

## Notes
- 8 papers reviewed — meets the guideline's "minimum 5" for literature review submission.
- FairFace (Karkkainen & Joo) is the only benchmark-dataset paper in `references.bib`; UTKFace is discussed in the report as a secondary benchmark but has no corresponding bib entry — no citable source paper currently tracked for it.
- Two non-paper regulatory sources (`euaiact2024`, `nist2023aiframework`) excluded from the matrix per the guideline's paper-only column spec; both are already cited directly in `intro.tex` and `systemArchitectureAndMethodology.tex`.
